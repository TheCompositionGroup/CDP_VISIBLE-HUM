# Seven-Night Screening Log

Print this. One row per night. Keep placement identical every night.

**Do not record a decibel level.** An uncalibrated phone cannot make a traceable
low-frequency SPL claim, and one bad number discredits the whole record.

### Language that keeps you credible

Use: *detected*, *consistent with*, *worth investigating*, *screening only*.
Avoid: *proves*, *caused by*, *in violation*, *measured at X dB*.
