## What this is

If you hear a persistent hum at night and can't prove it exists, this guide helps you produce a record of its **frequency, timing, duration, and recurrence** using a phone you already own, for **$0**.

It deliberately **does not** claim sound-pressure levels. An uncalibrated phone cannot make a traceable low-frequency dB claim, and one bad number discredits an otherwise sound record. Frequency and time are what consumer hardware does well. That's what this measures.

**Start here → [`docs/guide.md`](docs/guide.md)**

Sections 1–3 cost one evening, need no equipment, and may protect you from misdirected spending (replacement windows can cost $3,000–8,000, a rough guess). Section 4 costs seven nights and is only worth doing if you have named someone who will read the result. The guide says so in its first paragraph.

## Why the old revisions matter

The concept began as an attempt to convert data-center noise into electricity. It failed by nearly nine orders of magnitude, and the failure produced everything useful in the current guide.

- **v1 — Nothing to Harvest.** The arithmetic that closed the generator concept, plus the finding that every conversion pathway which closes technically pays the facility operator, not the resident. The result is summarized in Appendix A of the guide.
- **v3.** The hardware logger, withdrawn for calibration cost and prior art.
- **v2** exists only as working text.

The v1 and v3 files are **not in this repository yet**. Only their conclusions are kept here, in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md) and Appendix A of the guide.

A documented kill is a result.

## Help test this

You don't need to know anything about sound. We need people who hear a hum to try the guide and tell us where it broke.

**Please do only Sections 1–3 first.** They take one evening and cost $0. Don't start the seven-night part yet.

Then tell us:

- your phone model and its operating system
- the app you used, and its version
- the step where you got stuck
- what you expected to happen

**Two ways to tell us:**

- **On GitHub:** [open a field report](https://github.com/TheCompositionGroup/CDP_VISIBLE-HUM/issues/new?template=field-report.md)
- **No GitHub?** Email **bananas@fancyape.com**. Plain text is fine. Send the same four things.

Please don't send your home address or audio recordings in a public issue.
